# Illustration credits

Real book illustrations are bundled only when the artwork is in the public domain worldwide
(artist died more than 70 years ago). Each plate keeps its credit line in the app (player, "From the book").

## The Baby's Own Aesop (1887)
- Pictures: Walter Crane (1845-1915). Engraved and printed by Edmund Evans (1826-1905).
- Source scans: Project Gutenberg eBook #25433, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/25433
  (HTML with images, `pg25433-h.zip`; the Gutenberg page lists the release as public domain).
- Used for: The Fox and the Grapes (plate 9), The Wind and the Sun (11), The Lion and the Mouse (14),
  The Crow and the Pitcher (38), The Tortoise and the Hare (48).
- The plates are unmodified except for the crop used on story covers. The Gutenberg name and logo are not used.
- Raw downloads live on D: (`D:\DevData\downloads\crane-aesop`), not in the repo.

## Online books (not in the APK; hosted as GitHub Release assets, tag `content`)
Built by a script from Project Gutenberg HTML into `content/` (gitignored): `catalog.json` plus the images.
- **The Tale of Peter Rabbit** (1902): text and pictures by Beatrix Potter (d. 1943). Gutenberg #14838.
- **Grimm's Fairy Tales, illustrated by Arthur Rackham** (1909; reissued 1920 as Snowdrop & Other Tales). Rackham d. 1939.
  Gutenberg #37381. Stories used: Briar Rose, The White Snake, The Wolf and the Seven Kids, The Queen Bee,
  The Water of Life, The Goosegirl, The Seven Ravens, King Thrushbeard, The Golden Goose.
  Left out on purpose for a calm, family-safe app: Snowdrop and Ashenputtel (violent endings) and
  "The Jew among the Thorns" (antisemitic).
- Checked and rejected: Jacobs' English Fairy Tales on Gutenberg (#7439) has no illustrations in its HTML.
